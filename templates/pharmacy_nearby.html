Why this list can be trusted

Rows marked tie-up shop come from the nearby_pharmacies + nearby_pharmacy_stocks tables, which the pharmacy counter updates - so the number of packs you see is a stock figure, not a guess. Rows from Google are live chemist listings: they prove a shop is open nearby, but the stock has to be confirmed on the phone. Both kinds are cached in SQL, so the same search is instant - and still works - the next time.
